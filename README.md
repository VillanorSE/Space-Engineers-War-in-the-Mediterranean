# WW2: War in the Mediterranean

The Mediterranean, sometime in the early 1940s. Two navies contest the sea lanes. Two air forces trade blows over convoys and coastlines. Two armies dig in on the ground between them. This is a Space Engineers encounters mod built around that theater. Ships, aircraft, ground vehicles, and installations, all added to the world through Modular Encounters Systems.

**Gray** flies Italian colors: Regia Marina cruisers, Savoia-Marchetti bombers, Semovente tank destroyers rolling across North African sand. **Green** flies French: Dunkerque-class battleships, Lioré et Olivier bombers, tanks that fought and fell in 1940 and, in places, kept fighting after. Spawns reflect the historic vehicle lineups.

## Earn your fleet

Nobody starts with a battleship. Every ship, plane, tank, and base you build runs through a Core system that caps what you can field: a Corvette and a light truck to start, working up through Destroyers and Cruisers and eventually Carriers and Battleships as you prove yourself. Progress is the result of engaging with the world. Components salvaged from wrecks, looted from cargo, and vehicles stolen or purchased provide access to more powerful tools. Combat, salvage, and trade all feed the same progress, so there's no single way you have to play to get stronger.

## A world that remembers

Territory grows from each side's ports and airfields, and airfields change hands as it shifts. As the war escalates, fighters take to the air in flights of three that break off once two wingmen are down instead of fighting to the last plane. Attack a side's depots or factories and it sends a reprisal flight after you; build a base in hostile territory and expect air raids. A War News feed reports it all. Depots and outposts are planned to become capturable too. Standing with a faction shifts based on who gets hurt, not simply how much damage was done.

## Take what you can carry

Lightly defended garages and hangars provide a means for you to capture fully functional replica vehicles as an alternative to designing from scratch.

## Built to grow

The mod is built with room to grow. A custom Mediterranean coastline is one direction being explored for the world itself. Sub-factions are another: splitting Gray and Green into their historical constituent nations, with Germany under Gray and Britain under Green as the leading candidates, and the US as a further possibility down the line. The current roster reflects where things stand today rather than a finished picture, and any future additions get the same treatment as everything already in the mod: researched against the real history they're drawn from rather than picked for convenience.

## Repository structure

This repo covers three Workshop mods, developed together:

- **`WW2-WitM-MES/`** — Modular Encounters Systems content: spawn groups, behaviors, factions, loot.
- **`WW2-WitM-Framework/`** — mechanics and scenario systems: Ship Core progression, suit tweaks, respawn vehicles.
- **`WW2-WitM-Planet/`** — the Mediterranean planet: terrain, biomes and materials.
