# War in the Mediterranean: mod list

Add the mods in **Required** and **World rules** to your world (the official world already has them). Each one brings
in its own requirements, listed under **Pulled in automatically**, so you don't add those yourself.

## Required

| Mod | Workshop ID | What it does here |
|---|---|---|
| WW2-WitM-MES | 3793633400 | The encounters: ships, aircraft, ground vehicles, ports, airfields, garages and hangars. Brings in the Framework and every weapon and part pack the vehicles use. |
| WW2-WitM-Planet | 3788435958 | The Mediterranean planet with its historic locations and roads. |
| Fletcher Armaments [Weaponcore Edition] | 2844434226 | Naval guns on the warships. |
| (IAP) Inconvenient Additions Pack | 2883075089 | Blocks used by several vehicles. |
| ModAdjusterV2 | 3017795356 | Applies the Framework's tech costs (military components for weapons, propellers, wheels). |
| AiEnabled | 2596208372 | Bot crews. |

## World rules (part of the scenario)

| Mod | Workshop ID | What it does here |
|---|---|---|
| Suppress Vanilla Cargo Ships | 888457124 | No vanilla cargo ships. |
| Suppress Vanilla Encounters | 888457381 | No vanilla space/planet encounters. |
| Suppress Vanilla Planetary Installations | 3796756949 | No vanilla planetary installations. |
| Hostile Takeover | 2694751775 | Hacking and taking over grids. |
| Block Restrictions | 2053202808 | Keeps out-of-period blocks out of the G-menu. |
| AllianceRepHelper | 3660799548 | Pick your side: a faction leader types `/alliance GRAY` or `/alliance GREEN` to ally with one side and become the other's enemy. New player factions start hostile to both. |
